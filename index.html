Dr Thomas Jahn is a practitioner of Chinese medicine with a focus on addressing a broad variety of compromised health states, particularly physical injuries and chronic, stubborn conditions.

His oriental medicine journey began over thirty years ago whilst growing up in Japan, and continued with formal studies in the US and China. He returned to South Africa twenty years ago, and now practises from his home in Kommetjie and at 99 Kloof Street, Gardens on Thursdays.
